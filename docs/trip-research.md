### Lightning Lane model relevant to this trip

- Multi Pass allows up to three advance selections in one park.
- At Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, and Hollywood Studios, the initial choice set is grouped: at most one from the first group, with the other two from the second group or all three from the second group.
- Animal Kingdom has no initial groups.
- After redeeming the first Multi Pass selection, the guest can choose another available Multi Pass experience. With Park Hopper admission, that later choice can be in another park.
- A guest may purchase up to two Single Passes in a day, subject to availability.
- Seven Dwarfs Mine Train and TRON are Single Pass attractions at Magic Kingdom; Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind is Single Pass at EPCOT.

Official source: https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/en_CA/lightning-lane-passes/

### Trip-specific Lightning Lane checks

- September 20, Magic Kingdom: The Barnstormer and Haunted Mansion are Multi Pass second-group choices. Seven Dwarfs is a separate Single Pass. Add one first-group Multi Pass choice to use all three advance Multi Pass slots.
- September 21, Animal Kingdom: Expedition Everest, Na'vi River Journey, and Kilimanjaro Safaris can form the three advance Multi Pass selections; the two shows can be later options if available.
- September 23, Hollywood Studios: choose one desired first-group attraction for the initial set; place the other desired first-group attraction in a “book after first redemption” queue. Toy Story Mania and Tower of Terror can support the initial set under the current published grouping.
- September 24, EPCOT: Guardians is Single Pass. Frozen and Remy's are both first-group Multi Pass experiences, so only one can be in the initial three.
- September 25, Magic Kingdom: Big Thunder Mountain is a first-group Multi Pass choice; Seven Dwarfs and TRON can use the two allowed Single Pass purchases. Add two second-group Multi Pass choices if the family wants three advance Multi Pass selections.
- September 26, Magic Kingdom/check-out: Space Mountain is a first-group Multi Pass choice and TRON is Single Pass. The app should warn when park plans leave an unsafe buffer before the 5:13 PM MCO flight.
