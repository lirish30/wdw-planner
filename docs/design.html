Do’s and Don’ts

✓ Do

  • Keep the selected day and next action obvious.
  • Preserve imported family language and choices.
  • Pair every status color with explicit text.
  • Design for one-handed, outdoor mobile use.
  • Show applicability dates for changing rules.

✗ Don’t

  • Recreate the CSV as a scrolling grid.
  • Turn every fact into a bordered card.
  • Add decorative SVG animation or novelty type.
  • Imply booking capability or guaranteed availability.
  • Hide naps, buffers, or subgroup notes.
